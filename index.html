01 / Interaction

Grab a lime corner to pull the whole image. The pale points are Bezier controls: move them to tune the curve. You can drag them beyond the frame and make the picture fold in on itself.

This is a live mesh, not a filter. The video keeps playing underneath every bend. Press Reset to bring the surface home.
