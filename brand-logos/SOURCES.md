# Logo sources

The marks identify Tiago Pereira's stated past clients; trademarks belong to their respective owners. Display uses monochrome rendering, scanlines, and tracking distortion.

- SpaceX, Nike, Google, Lyft, Indeed: Simple Icons (CC0), https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons ; published SVG package via jsDelivr. SpaceX and Nike viewboxes cropped.
- Nickelodeon: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Nickelodeon_2009_logo.svg (public-domain textlogo classification).
- Comedy Central: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Comedy_Central_2018.svg (public-domain textlogo classification).
- Arc'Teryx: official inline wordmark at https://arcteryx.com/us/en/ ; fill changed to black.
- Ben & Jerry's: https://logos-download.com/89612-ben-jerrys-logo-download.html ; white lettering converted to transparent cutouts for monochrome display; vector https://wp.logos-download.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/Ben__Jerrys_Logo.svg .
